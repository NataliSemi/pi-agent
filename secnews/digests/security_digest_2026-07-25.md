**Link:** https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/slopsquatting-phantom-domains-and-hallusquatting-are-the-same-ai-attack/

**Relevance: High**

**Reason:** This article highlights the significant cybersecurity risk posed by AI-driven language models, specifically through the concept of "halluSquatting," which exploits the trust developers have in fake identifiers generated by these models. **Summary:**
- **Bullet 1:** AI coding agents can generate names that resemble legitimate commands, leading to vulnerabilities.
- **Bullet 2:** Developers often trust the names they receive without verifying their authenticity, making them susceptible to exploitation.

**Security+ Domain: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations**

**Key Terms:**
- AI
- Language Models
- HalluSquatting

**Defensive Lesson:**
- **Practical Defensive Takeaway:** Implement rigorous verification processes for the names generated by AI models to prevent attackers from exploiting these vulnerabilities.
