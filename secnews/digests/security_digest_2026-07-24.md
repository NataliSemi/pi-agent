**Reason:** This article is directly relevant to cybersecurity learners because it discusses the enforcement of EU antitrust laws, specifically focusing on Google's compliance with the Digital Markets Act. The fine imposed by the European Commission highlights the importance of maintaining fair competition and avoiding practices that could lead to market dominance or unfair advantages for a company.

**Summary:**

- **Bullet 1:** The European Commission fined Google €890 million ($1 billion) for violating the Digital Markets Act, which aims to ensure fair online competition.
  
- **Bullet 2:** Google was designated as a gatekeeper in September 2023 and faced non-compliance investigations opened in March 2024. The fine covers both boosting its services in search results and steering practices on the Google Play app store.

- **Bullet 3:** Google is ordered to end these DMA violations within 60 days or face penalty payments of up to 5% of its worldwide turnover, with the company potentially appealing today's decisions.
