## 23andMe to pay $18 million in new genetics data breach settlement

**Source:** BleepingComputer

**Published:** Thu, 16 Jul 2026 09:47:23 -0400

**Link:** https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/23andme-to-pay-18-million-in-new-genetics-data-breach-settlement/

**Relevance: High**

**Reason:** This article highlights the severe consequences of inadequate cybersecurity measures, specifically in handling sensitive genetic data. The $18 million settlement underscores the importance of robust security protocols to protect customer information from breaches and unauthorized access.

**Summary:**
- 23andMe agreed to pay $18 million to settle claims for a massive data breach that compromised the personal genetic information of over 6.9 million customers.
- The incident involved credential-stuffing attacks, which went unnoticed for five months, resulting in the theft of customer data and its sale on the dark web.
- The settlement includes new security requirements such as a data security advisory board, risk analysis protocols, and consumer rights to delete their data.

**Security+ Domain: 2. Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations**

**Key Terms:**
- Genetic testing company
- Data breach
- Credential-stuffing attacks
- Dark web

**Defensive Lesson:**
- Implement strong password policies and multifactor authentication.
- Use intrusion prevention systems to detect unusual login activity.
- Conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
