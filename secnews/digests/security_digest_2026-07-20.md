## Hackers abuse ViPNet software to target Russian govt agencies

**Source:** BleepingComputer

**Published:** Sun, 19 Jul 2026 10:23:46 -0400

**Link:** https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-abuse-vipnet-software-to-target-russian-govt-agencies/

**Relevance: High**

**Reason:** This article highlights the vulnerability of Russian government agencies and other high-value organizations to cyber threats, specifically targeting their use of ViPNet software. The threat actor is exploiting an update mechanism for this product suite to deploy malicious payloads that can compromise systems. This demonstrates a sophisticated method used by attackers to bypass security measures, making it crucial for cybersecurity learners to understand the importance of keeping software and systems up-to-date with patches and updates.

**Summary:**
- **Bullet 1:** The threat actor is abusing ViPNet’s update mechanism to deploy malicious payloads targeting Russian government agencies.
- **Bullet 2:** This campaign has impacted organizations in sectors including government, energy, transport, education, and logistics.
- **Bullet 3:** Kaspersky researchers have observed this campaign since May 2025, with the latest attack involving a malicious DLL file (HelloInjector) that injects into svchost.exe.

**Security+ Domain: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations**

**Key Terms:**
- **Term 1:** ViPNet
- **Term 2:** Malware Injection
- **Term 3:** Command-and-Control (C2)

**Defensive Lesson:**
- Ensure all software is regularly updated to protect against vulnerabilities exploited by attackers.
