## CISA warns of cyberattacks disrupting U.S. water utilities

**Published:** Fri, 31 Jul 2026 12:49:49 -0400

**Link:** https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-warns-of-cyberattacks-disrupting-us-water-utilities/

**Relevance: High**

**Reason:** This article highlights the significant threat to critical infrastructure, specifically water and wastewater systems, due to internet-exposed programmable logic controllers (PLCs). The alert from CISA underscores the importance of securing operational technology (OT) assets, which are often overlooked in cybersecurity practices. This relevance is high because it directly impacts organizations that manage these crucial utilities, emphasizing the need for immediate action.

**Summary:**
- **Bullet 1:** CISA warns of hackers targeting internet-exposed PLCs and disrupting water systems.
- **Bullet 2:** Organizations should remove exposed OT assets from direct internet exposure or use secure connections like VPNs.
- **Bullet 3:** Default passwords should be changed, access limited to IP address allow-lists.

**Security+ Domain: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations**

**Key Terms:**
- Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs)
- Internet Exposed Assets
- Operational Technology (OT)

**Defensive Lesson:**
- **Practical Takeaway:** Implement strong password policies and use secure access methods to protect OT assets.
