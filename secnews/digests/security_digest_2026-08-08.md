The North Carolina Ports' response demonstrates the need for robust security measures, including active monitoring and preparedness plans.

### Summary:

1. **Port Cyberattack**: A cyberattack on the North Carolina Ports disrupted IT systems at three facilities (Wilmington, Morehead City, and Charlotte Inland Port), causing a systems-wide outage that delayed port operations.
2. **Detection and Response**: The attack was detected on August 4, with an activation of the cybersecurity contingency plan starting on August 5. Gates reopened at 8 AM on August 5, leading to delays in port operations and trucking.

## Real emails, hijacked payments: Two H1 2026 attack chains

**Source:** BleepingComputer

**Published:** Fri, 07 Aug 2026 10:00:10 -0400

**Link:** https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/real-emails-hijacked-payments-two-h1-2026-attack-chains/

**Relevance: High**

**Reason:** This article highlights the sophistication and diversification of cyberattacks, demonstrating how attackers are using legitimate accounts and blockchain data to bypass traditional security measures. Understanding these methods is crucial for learners in cybersecurity as it shows that even trusted systems can be compromised.

**Summary:**
- **Bullet 1:** The banking-malware campaign started with a compromised corporate mailbox and ended with proxy and browser manipulation.
- **Bullet 2:** A cryptocurrency campaign used a Rust-based clipper to retrieve command-and-control infrastructure pointers from Binance Smart Chain, changing the destination address locally before executing the transaction.
- **Bullet 3:** Both campaigns showed that attackers could manipulate legitimate accounts and blockchain data without breaking trusted systems.

**Security+ domain:**
- **Key terms:**
  - Banking-malware
  - Clipper
  - Command-and-control infrastructure

**Defensive lesson:**
- Recognize the importance of multi-factor authentication (MFA) to prevent attackers from using compromised accounts.
- Be aware that even legitimate email addresses can be used as a front for malicious activities.

### Security+ Practice Question

**Question:** Which Security+ concept helps organizations choose controls based on likelihood and impact?

A) Risk management
B) Password sharing
C) Disabling logs
D) Ignoring vulnerabilities

**Correct answer:** A

**Explanation:** Risk management helps prioritize controls based on business impact and threat likelihood.
