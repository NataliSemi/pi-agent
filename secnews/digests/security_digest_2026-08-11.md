**Reason:** This article highlights a significant security vulnerability in the infrastructure of a popular WordPress plugin provider, which could have been exploited by an attacker to compromise thousands of websites. The use of cross-site scripting (XSS) and other vulnerabilities indicates a high risk of exploitation for legitimate users.

**Summary:**
- **BdThemes Plugins Supply-chain Hack Creates Rogue WordPress Admins**: A threat actor compromised the upstream infrastructure of BdThemes, a developer of premium WordPress web-design tools, by modifying a remote JSON feed delivered to administrators' browsers. This resulted in rogue admin accounts being created on affected websites.
- **Affected Products and Active Installations**: The impacted products include Element Pack, Prime Slider, Ultimate Post Kit, Pixel Gallery, and Ultimate Store Kit. BdThemes provides over 350,000 active installs of its flagship free Element Pack plugin alone.

**Security+ Domain: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations**

**Key Terms:**
- XSS (Cross-Site Scripting)
- JSON Response Parsing
- Web Application Firewall (WAF)

**Defensive Lesson:**
- **Implement Regular Security Audits**: Ensure that all plugins are regularly reviewed for vulnerabilities.
- **Use Strong Authentication Mechanisms**: Implement multi-factor authentication to prevent unauthorized access even if credentials are compromised.

**Relevance: High**

**Reason:** This incident highlights the vulnerabilities in operational technology (OT) networks, which are often overlooked compared to more visible cybersecurity threats. The use of private Access Point Names (APNs) and misconfigurations can lead to unauthorized access, compromising critical infrastructure like heat treatment systems and steam turbines.

**Summary:**
- **Bullet 1:** Hackers used a dedicated mobile gateway to compromise a second facility during the destructive cyberattacks that hit Poland's energy sector last year.
- **Bullet 2:** The attacker exploited misconfigured private Access Point Names (APNs) to access the operational technology network, leading to shutdowns of heat treatment systems and steam turbines at a small combined heat-and-power plant.
- **Bullet 3:** Despite no impact on grid operations or population, this incident underscores the importance of securing OT networks against potential threats.

**Security+ domain: Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations**

**Key terms:**

- Operational Technology (OT)
- Distributed Energy Resource (DER)

**Defensive lesson:**
